7 · The amber / violet convention

Amber = verified local data read from the user's files. Violet = AI-inferred or low-confidence data. Every inferred value carries a leading ~ from .gp-inferred and must also carry a title naming its source. This is a functional rule, not a decorative one — pick the pair by provenance.
